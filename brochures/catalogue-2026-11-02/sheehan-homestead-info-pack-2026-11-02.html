Who we areSheehan Homestead · Callahan, FL
A real family farm in Nassau County

Hi, we're the Sheehans.

Sheehan Homestead is a small, educationally focused family farm on the edge of Callahan, Florida. Our mission is simple: create unique experiences and learning opportunities while spreading joy. We bring our friendly goats, bunnies and chickens to your community, school or event, and we welcome families, co-ops and groups to visit us on the farm.

Momtrepreneur & educator

Ann-Marie Sheehan, M.Ed.

Co-owner and the heart of our programming. Ann-Marie spent 10+ years as a New Jersey public-school educator before bringing her classroom outdoors. She holds an M.Ed. in STEM Education Leadership and a BA in Geological & Earth Sciences, and she leads STEM on Call, Mommy & Me and our homeschool programs.

Founder & your planning partner

Michael Sheehan

Co-owner. Michael leads sales and planning, so directors have one reliable point of contact from the first call to load-out. He is the one who will help you map out your whole year.

Our givingSheehan Homestead · Callahan, FL
Our giving philosophy

Give Twice. Our ad budget goes to you.

Most companies spend seven to twelve percent of what they earn on advertising. We set aside that same share and give it away instead, to the families, schools, churches and causes that make our community worth living in. Our rule is simple: give at least twice as much as you receive. We don't buy billboards. We give, and the word travels from people who know who did a nice thing.

Gold membership · worth over $100

We give Gold away.

  • A free farm tour for two
  • A $25 farm gift
  • About 40% off camp, the mobile zoo and field trips
  • Access to our app

We hand Gold memberships to churches, schools and nonprofits to auction, raffle 50/50, or give to a family who needs a lift.

Named for our daughter. Schools, churches and qualifying nonprofits book the full experience at about 60% off our list price, confirmed per date. The difference is our gift to your cause.

Logistics at a glance

  • Space: about 15-20 ft x 15-20 ft. We are light and mobile, and we once fit between two palm trees by a pre-K door.
  • Surface: grass or dirt is best. Parking lot or concrete only? A hay fee of $30-$50 applies. A tarp is available when needed.
  • Arrival: we arrive about 30 minutes early and set up in 10-15 minutes. Need us earlier? An early-arrival fee applies.
  • Tax: CDDs are usually tax-exempt. Please send your certificate.
  • Travel: quoted by distance from Callahan; the furthest trips are typically about 1 hour each way. Partners save 50%.
  • Weather: free weather reschedule. Reschedule or cancel with at least 48 hours notice for refund options (admin fee may apply).
