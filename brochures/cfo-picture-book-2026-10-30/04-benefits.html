Rung 4 of 7 · Benefits

Once payroll runs,
you get benefits that keep good people.

Benefits are how a small farm keeps the helper who already knows every goat by name. They also lower the tax bill on money you would spend anyway.
From your CFO
Owners first, staff second. Run your own premiums through payroll the day payroll starts. Add a QSEHRA or ICHRA the day your first non owner goes on W-2. Pick Solo 401(k) while it's just you two, SIMPLE IRA once helpers join.

Plain words

Owner health premiums
The business pays your health premiums, they show on your W-2 as wages without payroll tax, and you deduct them on your return. Spouses count together as more than 2% owners.
QSEHRA
A monthly allowance that repays staff for their own health premiums. For employers under 50 people with no group plan. 2026 caps: $6,450 single, $13,100 family.
ICHRA
The same idea with no dollar cap, and it grows with you. Neither is tax free for more than 2% owners.
SIMPLE IRA
An easy retirement plan for small employers. 2026: employees save up to $17,000, plus $4,000 more at 50 and over. You match up to 3%.
Solo 401(k)
A retirement plan for owners and spouses only, with no other employees. 2026 savings limit: $24,500.

Your edge on this rung

  • You come from enterprise architecture and compliance software. You already know how to map red tape and sell it. Use that here.
  • If you sit the Enrolled Agent exam (the IRS tax credential), the testing window you noted closes Feb 28, 2027.
  • Small group health plans in Florida usually need at least one non owner on W-2. Owner only groups often get declined.
  • Put every benefit choice in a one page board resolution. Lenders and investors like seeing it.
