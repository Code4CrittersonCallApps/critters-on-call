Enterprise buyers check paperwork before they check prices. Clean paperwork is the moat. It is the first thing reporters, competitors and franchise buyers look at.
From your CFO
Settle the USDA question first. Run USDA's free online Licensing Assistant with your real headcount of animals. It takes minutes, and it is the number one thing a reporter or a competitor checks.

Plain words

USDA exhibitor license (Class C)
The federal license for showing animals to the public. USDA says petting zoos with regulated animals need one. Exempt: only farm animals at agricultural fairs, or 8 or fewer animals kept for exhibition. A Standard zoo brings 9 or more.
FWC permits
Florida wildlife permits. Needed for captive wildlife and exotic species, not for domestic goats, bunnies or chickens.
Health certificate (OCVI)
A vet's signed health paper. Florida wants one within 90 days, plus official ID, for goats at an exhibition.
Business tax receipt
The local license to do business. Nassau County dropped its county receipt for unincorporated areas. A town may still require one inside its limits.
1099 contractor
Someone who runs their own work and gets a 1099 form, not a paycheck. The IRS looks at who controls how the work gets done.

The compliance checklist

  • Helper standards in writing. Helpers accept or decline each gig. Flat rate per gig, posted before they accept. You set the result, they bring their skill. Collect a W-9 before the first payout.
  • 1099 forms. For payments made in 2026, file a 1099-NEC for anyone you pay $2,000 or more in the year (it was $600).
  • Waivers signed at check in, with property rules spelled out. Have your lawyer review the wording.
  • Vehicle. Personal auto policies often exclude business use. Ask your agent for commercial auto on anything that hauls animals.
  • Sunbiz annual report by May 1. Sales tax registration if you sell admissions or merchandise.
  • Animal Control permit kept current, and the farm's operating record documented.

Once you have this, you get

  • CDD, school and corporate vendor approval on the first try
  • Sponsor checks with your name on the season
  • Press without surprises
  • A clean file for a franchise disclosure later
