We come to you

Mobile petting zoos

The farm, delivered to your lawn. Gentle goats, bunnies and fowl in a fenced pen.

  • Mini, Standard, and Standard plus a Full Side of seasonal babies
  • Fall fests, spring flings, pool days, school days, birthdays
  • We arrive early and set up in minutes
Wellness

Goat yoga

A relaxed, all-levels session with our goats wandering, nibbling and sometimes climbing on board.

  • At your community lawn or private on our farm
  • Boozy goat yoga for adult groups
Learning

STEM on Call

Hands-on science and engineering challenges led by Ann-Marie Sheehan, M.Ed., a former public-school educator.

  • Bridge, tower, flight and design builds
  • Nature and farm science lessons
  • Often paired with an animal visit
Ages 0 to 5

Mommy & Me

A gentle hour outdoors for little ones and their grown-ups: baby goats, sensory play and a calm farm stroll.

  • On the farm Thursdays, 10:00 to 11:00 AM, and select Saturdays
  • Also available at your clubhouse or lawn
Families & co-ops

Homeschool programs

Nature, farm and STEM lessons with Ann-Marie on our Callahan farm, from single classes to co-op field trips.

  • Step Up For Students reimbursement available for eligible families
Come to the farm

Visit Us

Spend a morning on the homestead at 44065 Cushman Road, Callahan.

  • Everyone signs a waiver on entry
  • Please leave pets at home
