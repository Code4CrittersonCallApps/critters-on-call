Golden Goatie targets

The golden goat is Sheehan Homestead’s calling card, in a form nobody throws away. Same award we already give to GOAT parents — now for local business GOATs. A thank-you, not a gimmick.

Draft only. Nothing has been sent. November 19, 2026 · America/New_York. Fifteen superconnectors first, then forty sponsor targets. Draft only.

The process

  1. Step 1, this week. Walk in. Hand them a Golden Goatie and a handwritten note. Offer one genuine compliment. No ask.
  2. Step 2, one to two weeks later. Leave a one-page snapshot of their business, plus the offer: free to join, or start at $25.

Track A — dealership GMs and big owners — gets the goat and the handwritten note only.

Track B — everyone else on the sponsor list — also gets the short letter below, so they know what this is.

Superconnectors — same drop as Track A. A thank-you, not a sponsor ask.

Walk-in

Hi, I’m Michael with Sheehan Homestead in Callahan. We give Golden Goaties to GOAT parents. You’ve been a GOAT to this community, so this one’s for you. Just a thank-you.

30 words. Leave the card with the goat. Do not pitch.

The handwritten note

Under 20 words. Use their name. No ask, no review, no strings.

[Name], we give Golden Goaties to GOAT parents. Thank you for being a GOAT to this community.

Suggested lines for each target are in the table and the CSV. For the top 10, use the specific compliment on the row.

Track B letter

Typed, low-pressure, under 90 words. Leave it with the handwritten note, not instead of it.

Hi [Name], I’m Michael with Sheehan Homestead in Callahan. We give Golden Goaties to GOAT parents — and to local businesses that show up for this community. This one is yours, with our thanks. No strings. If you’d ever like to be part of our family events, sponsorship is free to join, or starts at $25. Cheers, Michael

Step 2 snapshot

One page. Their name at the top. Then four facts, and the offer.

  • Google rating and review count
  • Local search visibility (Maps and the local pack)
  • AI-search visibility (how they show up when families ask an assistant)
  • Community footprint (sponsorships, events, mentions)

Follow-up note:

Hi [Name] — Michael at Sheehan Homestead. I dropped a Golden Goatie by a couple of weeks ago and wanted to leave you a one-page snapshot of how your business shows up online and around town. If you’d like to be part of our family events, sponsorship is free to join, or starts at $25. Cheers, Michael.

Why a trophy, not a card

Leave Track A with the handwritten note only. A typed pitch in the same visit turns a thank-you into a brochure.

Dimensional gifts already beat flat letters. The DMA’s 2010 report put dimensional mail at a 5.72% response to existing customers, versus 3.42% for letter-size envelopes. The 2015 report put dimensional prospect mail at a 2.8% median, versus 1% for letters. A Baylor test of 3,000 school administrators found a boxed promotional product pulled 3.3%, versus 1.9% for a sales letter alone.

Walk the goat in. It is lumpy mail a receptionist is unlikely to toss. Track B can add the short letter so they know sponsorship is free, or starts at $25. Keep that offer off the handwritten note.

A Local GOAT badge they can display

Businesses already hang trust awards and post the win. Bizrate tells Circle of Excellence winners to put the badge on their site, in ads and on social, and to request a paper certificate for the store. Yelp mails a “People Love Us on Yelp” window cling. BBB issues Accredited Business seals for the door. Folio Weekly gives Best of Jax winners a graphics package, and shops hang the plaques.

Give a free Local GOAT certificate and a simple badge they can frame or post. No fee. No review ask. The trophy is the thing on the shelf; the badge is what they share.
