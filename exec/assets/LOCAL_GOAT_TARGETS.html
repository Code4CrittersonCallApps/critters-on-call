Local GOAT targets

We're honoring local businesses that serve the same families we do. Twenty-five walk-in stops around Callahan, Nassau, Jacksonville and St. Johns. Ranked for reposts first, then a plausible ad or booking, then a gift that gets passed on. Draft only. Nothing has been sent.

Account-based field marketing: a hand-delivered thank-you that works like a pricey business card. Public sources only. Age and private interests are listed only when published; otherwise “not public.” No invented stats.

A · 12 stops · defaultBronze Hall of Fame goat + handwritten note with your card taped in + the small Who-we-are card. Low effort, high delivery odds.
B · 8 stops · comicsEverything in A, plus a manila envelope with 3 Superman comics and the re-gift slip. Valued as a ~$25 gift. Daycares, coffee counters, the feed store, kids' desks.
C · 5 stops · STEM kitEverything in A, plus the $25 STEM kit, delivered by name: “This is a personal gift. Please make sure [Name] gets it.” Philanthropy-known owners only.

The note, by hand, on your stationery

Sign every one Cheers, Michael. Tape your card in.

  1. Thanks for being a local GOAT! We're honoring Jax businesses that serve families like we do.
  2. Thanks for being a local GOAT! We're honoring Jacksonville businesses that serve families like we do — and you made the list.
  3. Thanks for being a local GOAT! From our small-acre homestead in Callahan, we're honoring Jax businesses that serve families like we do.
