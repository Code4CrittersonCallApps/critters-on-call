Same stages as a 3% loan, repaid from license fees and On Call Brands revenue. Anything unpaid at month [24] converts at the $750k cap with a [1.25×] stock multiplier. Example: $150k unpaid → $187.5k → 25%.

Money

  • On Call Brands pays Sheehan Homestead a modest fixed site fee
  • Sheehan Homestead is paid separately for livestock, labor, equipment, and services
  • On Call Brands collects technology, gaming, subscription, and franchise revenue
  • Ownership splits apply to On Call Brands only

Partner gets / does not get

  • An On Call Brands stake (20% at the full SAFE, never over 49%) and its franchise economics
  • Proper governance + financial transparency
  • Contractual access to the model location and the tech
  • Land, homestead equity, or property rights
  • Livestock, farm equipment, or the mortgage
  • 49% of the existing farm business

Timeline

  • 0–18 mo: prove the operating model

Attorneys confirm before signing

  • Brand transition: farm moves off the family name (e.g., Jax Homestead); trademark the On Call marks

Recommendation

A 10–15 page commercial agreement package, not a real-estate or asset restructuring.

Principle

The partner buys into the business that scales, not the property that makes the first location possible.
