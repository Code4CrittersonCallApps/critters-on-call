Critters on Call · Deal sheet · Nov 22, 2026

The brass-tacks deal

Don't touch the farm. Create a separate 51/49 operating company. Give it contractual access to what it needs. That's it.

Unchanged

Sheehan FarmCo

Keeps its existing ownership, land, livestock, equipment, and mortgage.

51% Sheehan · 49% partner

Sheehan TechCo (Michael Sheehan, founder)

Runs the software, games, brand, and franchise business.

Five agreements. No asset transfers.

Land18-month site use of 3 acres. Then a 3-yr renewal option, then a mutually agreed 2-yr extension
TechnologyLicense to use the software, games, and digital infrastructure
CustomersAccess to the list, CRM, bookings, and relationships, subject to privacy and consent
Farm assetsUse of livestock, facilities, and equipment as needed. FarmCo keeps ownership
Brand / franchiseLicense to run the Sheehan model and build the franchise system

Money

  • TechCo pays FarmCo a modest fixed site fee
  • FarmCo is paid separately for livestock, labor, equipment, and services
  • TechCo collects technology, gaming, subscription, and franchise revenue
  • 51/49 applies to TechCo economics only

Partner gets / does not get

  • 49% of TechCo and its franchise economics
  • Proper governance + financial transparency
  • Contractual access to the model location and the tech
  • Land, homestead equity, or property rights
  • Livestock, farm equipment, or the mortgage
  • 49% of the existing farm business

Timeline

0–18 monthsProve the operating model
18 mo – 5 yrsExercise renewal rights and scale
Year 5Renew, relocate, expand, or restructure

Attorneys confirm before signing

  • Florida homestead, property tax, and mortgage implications
  • Whether the land arrangement is legally a license or a lease
  • Franchise disclosure and registration requirements

Recommendation

A 10–15 page commercial agreement package, not a real-estate or asset restructuring.

Principle

The partner buys into the business that scales, not the property that makes the first location possible.

Discussion draft. Not an offer to sell securities. Final terms are set in signed agreements.
