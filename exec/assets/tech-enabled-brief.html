Where the idea comes from: UK blue-collar consolidation

Franchise Brands plc (London AIM-listed; Metro Rod drains, Pirtek hoses, and others) lists "Technology-enabled business: we use technology to enhance the natural operational gearing of our franchise business." It runs nearly 600 franchise partners and £435m in system sales on one CRM (HubSpot), one finance system, and one works-management platform. In the US, PE-backed Zephyr rolls up local HVAC, plumbing, and electrical shops by "supporting them with superior operational processes, technology, and talent development." Same play, different trade. Ours is agritainment.
