  • Contracts Until reinstated, the company is limited to winding up. Counterparties can question authority to sign or perform.

  • Insurance Carriers often require the entity to be active. Confirm coverage after you reinstate, and keep a certificate of status.
  • Investors Diligence will show INACTIVE. That is a stop-sign until the record is clean.
  • CDD / vendors Vendor packets typically need a current certificate of status / good standing.
