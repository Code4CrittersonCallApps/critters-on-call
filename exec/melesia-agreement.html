Independent Sales Representative Agreement

Between Sheehan Homestead LLC, Callahan, Florida ("Sheehan Homestead") and Melesia [last name] ("Rep"). Effective date: [date].

  1. Role. Rep is an independent sales representative and the company's growth engine, working as BDR manager and senior account rep. Rep finds and books customers for Sheehan Homestead services and manages the accounts Rep books.
  2. Independent contractor (1099). Rep is an independent contractor, not an employee, partner or agent. Rep sets Rep's own hours and methods and uses Rep's own phone, computer and car. Sheehan Homestead does not withhold taxes or provide wages, benefits or workers' compensation. Rep provides a signed W-9 before the first payment, and Sheehan Homestead issues a Form 1099 as required. Rep is responsible for Rep's own taxes.
  3. Commission. One time events (list or Violet): 20% of the event price the customer pays, with a $100 minimum per event, plus $50 when Rep chooses to work the event as B2C marketing and the deal supports it. Recurring accounts: $25 for every visit the customer pays for, for as long as the account stays active, plus half of any amount above the floor price ($150 monthly, $200 quarterly).
  4. When commission is paid. Commission is earned and paid only when Sheehan Homestead is paid by the customer (deposit, prepayment or payment after delivery). Refunded or unpaid amounts earn no commission, and commission already paid on a refund is deducted from the next payment. Commission is paid within [14] days after the customer's payment clears.
  5. Pricing authority. Rep may quote list or target prices and discount down to the floor prices in the Melesia Rules. Anything lower, any custom work and any deal over $5,000 needs Michael's written approval. Sheehan Homestead confirms all dates and sends all invoices; Rep does not collect payment.
  6. No lifting or animal handling. Rep sells and may host Bunny Haven visits if Rep chooses. Rep does not lift, carry or handle animals; trained helpers do all animal handling.
  7. Leads and ownership. Leads, lists, customer records and materials provided by Sheehan Homestead belong to Sheehan Homestead. Rep calls only the leads provided. If Rep brings an organization Rep already knows, Rep tells Michael first, and once approved in writing it is credited to Rep. Rep returns or deletes lead lists when this agreement ends.
  8. House accounts excluded. Current customers, accounts Michael is already working, and accounts Michael designates as house accounts (for example sponsors and enterprise deals) are excluded and earn no commission unless Michael agrees in writing.
  9. Ending the agreement. Either party may end this agreement at any time by written notice, including text or email. Commission on bookings Rep made before the end date is still paid when the customer pays. Recurring residuals after the end date: [Michael to decide, for example 90 days].
  10. Conduct. Rep represents Sheehan Homestead honestly, follows the price sheet, does not promise dates or animals before Michael confirms, and keeps customer information private.
