# Elevator pitch and 5-minute teaser

**Critters on Call · Sheehan Homestead · Speaker script · November 24, 2026**

Use the 15-second line in a hallway. Use the ~45-second elevator when you have one breath more. Use the 5-minute teaser as a sit-down software demo, with [DEMO_SLIDES.html?v=1122b](assets/DEMO_SLIDES.html?v=1122b) on screen. Speak it confident and warm, in your own rhythm, plain words, no hedging.

Nothing here is a send. Sponsorship is the first ask. The SAFE is only if they want in.

Tech-enabled means I take the rigor I learned at PE- and VC-backed companies and apply it to any business. We started on our homestead. Look at the result. I can do this quickly, anywhere.

## 5-minute teaser demo

Open the deck first: [DEMO_SLIDES.html?v=1122b](assets/DEMO_SLIDES.html?v=1122b). Click, arrows, space, or Enter. `A` jumps to the asks (slide 21). `T` jumps to the tech anchor (slide 16). Have a second tab ready for the live Gold app, the wheel, and the locked search prompt. Do not open the booking tools, the dunk lists, or the dialer. If you open Growth, do not linger — money and pipeline figures are live on that page; slide 17 is the safe screenshot.

The season is the frame, not the product: Black Friday, Cyber Week, and the Christmas run-up. Kits, petting zoos, flyers. The package keeps getting better.

### Beat 1 · Hook — 0:00–0:40

**Show:** Slide 1 (opening), then stay with your face more than the slide.

**Say:**

This is the holiday window. We're selling kits, we're selling petting zoos, we're working the flyers, and as we round this out the package keeps getting better.

What does tech-enabled mean? It means I can apply the rigor I learned at PE- and VC-backed companies to literally any business. I built a lead-gen department to about 120 meetings a month. I sold to Fortune 37 through Fortune 500. We started with agritainment because I happen to own a homestead, and my wife happens to be a teacher. Look at what I was able to do for her.

### Beat 2 · Why agritainment / who we are — 0:40–1:20

**Show:** Slide 6 (the reveal), then slide 7 (Ann-Marie), then slide 9 (complementary founders). If you have ten seconds, glance at slide 10 (timeline).

**Say:**

That's us. Ann-Marie is a teacher and a co-founder. The farm was our idea. She wrote the STEM curriculum. I brought the GTM, the systems, and the software.

First customer was a Montessori school, November 2023 — STEM on Call. That's about three years from first customer to today. The mobile petting zoo came after, on our small-acre homestead in Callahan.

### Beat 3 · Look at this — 1:20–3:20

This is the demo. Point at the screen. One proof, then the next. Do not hedge. If a live search doesn't show us first, narrate what is on the screen and move.

**1:20–1:50 · Reviews and reach.** **Show:** Slide 15 (momentum). Optionally click through to Google or Facebook and come right back.

**Say:**

Look at the result. A hundred and sixteen five-star Google reviews. Almost 10,000 Facebook followers. In the last 28 days alone, 200,000-plus views, about 11,000 engagements, and about 2,500 new followers. That is a local farm business, built with the same rigor I'd put on a software book of business.

**1:50–2:20 · Institutional clients.** **Show:** Slide 14 (public proof). Name the institutions. Don't invent a series or a headcount the review doesn't support.

**Say:**

And it isn't only birthday parties. Wildlight CDD. Early Learning Coalition of Duval — they've booked us more than once and called us flexible and easy to work with. Marsh Landing Country Club. Safariland. A pediatric medical day care in St. Johns — 24 patients on that visit. That's institutional proof, on the books.

**2:20–2:50 · Blind search.** **Show:** Slide 5 (live search). If you run it live, use the locked pair, statewide first, then Jacksonville: *Top rated mobile goat yoga and mobile petting zoo in Florida.* Then the same pair for Jacksonville. Never run petting zoo alone. Then cut to slide 6.

**Say:**

Here's the taste test. Same prompt, cold, no login. When the query is mobile goat yoga and mobile petting zoo, we come back number one in Jacksonville. That's the engine sitting under the brand.

**2:50–3:20 · The product in software.** **Show:** Slide 17 (Growth, the safe screenshot), then live [giving-wheel](giving-wheel/) or [gold.html](gold.html), then slide 18 (ops) or [helper-onboarding.html](helper-onboarding.html). Optional: [track.html](track.html) — click Run live demo. Do not linger on live Growth.

**Say:**

This is the stack. Dashboards for the book of business. The Gold app. The prize wheel. The helper onboarding so we can run eight gigs at once. This is what "tech-enabled" looks like on a homestead operation — and this is why it repeats.

### Beat 4 · The repeatable model — 3:20–4:15

**Show:** Slide 12 (ecosystem / flywheel), then slide 16 (tech anchor).

**Say:**

We started here because we live here. The point is not that every business has goats. The point is the model: a vertically integrated ecosystem that feeds itself, born on software, not bolted on later. I can point this rigor at any business, and I can do it quickly.

### Beat 5 · The ask — 4:15–5:00

**Show:** Slide 21 (the asks). If they lean in, open [DEAL_SHEET.html?v=1122b](assets/DEAL_SHEET.html?v=1122b).

**Say:**

Here's how to come in. The first ask is a sponsorship — ride this Black Friday, Cyber Week, and Christmas run-up with us. If you have a business, there's advertising in the return. If you're a philanthropist, you're welcome too.

If you want a stake, it's a simple SAFE into On Call Brands — Critters on Call, STEM on Call, Exec MBA on Call. Up to $150,000, a $750,000 post-money cap, a 20% discount. At the cap that's 20%. I keep 80%, and I never go below 51%. The farm stays untouched. This funds formation and access, not a buyout.

I'd love to have you with us this season.
