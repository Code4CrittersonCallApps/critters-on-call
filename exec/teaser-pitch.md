# Elevator pitch and 5-minute teaser

**Critters on Call · Sheehan Homestead · Speaker script · November 24, 2026 · ?v=1124b**

This is a pitch for a **tech-enabled experiences and consulting firm, with an exit.** Use the 15-second line in a hallway. Use the ~45-second elevator when you have one breath more. Use the 5-minute teaser as a sit-down software demo, with [DEMO_SLIDES.html?v=1122b](assets/DEMO_SLIDES.html?v=1122b) on screen. Speak it confident and warm, in your own rhythm, plain words, no hedging.

Caveman path, both cuts: big ocean → trophy case → the bad-accountant story → not a one-trick pony → enterprise vision → exit → the ask. Sponsorship is the first ask. The SAFE is if they want in. The homestead retreat is the partnership option. Nothing here is a send.

Leave the personal origin for the long form only. Do not name competitors. Do not open the comparison slide (11). Do not mention any entity-status issue.

---

## 15-second one-liner

We're building a tech-enabled experiences and consulting firm, with an exit, in a big underserved market. We started on our homestead, with my wife and a few friends. Look at the traction. I can do this quickly, anywhere.

## 45-second elevator

We're a tech-enabled experiences and consulting firm in a big, underserved market — with an exit. Call this person, ask this question, they'll give you this answer.

The trophy case is traction: a hundred and sixteen five-star Google reviews, almost 10,000 Facebook followers, 200,000-plus views in the last 28 days, number one in a blind Jacksonville search. Three years, no outside money — just my wife, a few friends, and PE- and VC-backed rigor.

A year ago people laughed at the consolidation idea. Perpetual-license companies lost to SaaS on unit economics. We bring the good accountant.

This holiday season, start as a sponsor. Want in? A simple SAFE, or live on the homestead as franchise partner zero.

## 5-minute teaser demo

Open the deck first: [DEMO_SLIDES.html?v=1122b](assets/DEMO_SLIDES.html?v=1122b). Click, arrows, space, or Enter. `A` jumps to the asks (slide 21). `T` jumps to the tech anchor (slide 16). ### Beat 1 · Big ocean — 0:00–0:40

**Show:** Slide 2 (the market).

**Say:**

We're building a tech-enabled experiences and consulting firm — with an exit. This is the holiday window, and the package keeps getting better. But zoom out.

Jacksonville is 1.8 million people, the largest city by land area in the contiguous U.S., up 11% since 2020. Florida is number two in people added. A $129 billion GDP — fourth in Florida, 39th in the country. Family households, employer businesses, kids under 18. That's a slice of a bigger, underserved market. The research is the kind you can check yourself: call this person, ask this question, they'll give you this answer. A new perspective is what transforms a market.

We started with agritainment because I own a homestead and my wife Ann-Marie is a teacher. The firm is bigger than the farm.

### Beat 2 · Trophy case — 0:40–2:00

This is the demo. The most valuable thing in the case is traction. Point at the screen. One proof, then the next. Do not hedge. If a live search doesn't show us first, narrate what is on the screen and move.

**0:40–1:05 · Who built it, how fast.** **Show:** Slide 7 (Ann-Marie), then slide 10 (timeline).

**Say:**

Ann-Marie is a teacher and a co-founder. The farm was our idea. She wrote the STEM curriculum. I brought the GTM, the systems, and the software — the same rigor I learned at PE- and VC-backed companies. I built a lead-gen department to about 120 meetings a month, and I sold to Fortune 37 through Fortune 500.

First customer was a Montessori school, November 2023 — STEM on Call. About three years from first customer to today, on our small-acre homestead in Callahan.

**1:05–1:25 · Reviews and reach.** **Show:** Slide 15 (momentum). Optionally click through to Google or Facebook and come right back.

**Say:**

Look at the result. A hundred and sixteen five-star Google reviews. Almost 10,000 Facebook followers. In the last 28 days alone, 200,000-plus views, about 11,000 engagements, and about 2,500 new followers.

**1:25–1:45 · Institutional clients.** **Show:** Slide 14 (public proof). Name the institutions. Don't invent a series or a headcount the review doesn't support.

**Say:**

And it isn't only birthday parties. Wildlight CDD. Early Learning Coalition of Duval — they've booked us more than once and called us flexible and easy to work with. Marsh Landing Country Club. Safariland. A pediatric medical day care in St. Johns — 24 patients on that visit.

**1:45–2:00 · Blind search.** **Show:** Slide 5 (live search). If you run it live, use the locked pair, statewide first, then Jacksonville: *Top rated mobile goat yoga and mobile petting zoo in Florida.* Then the same pair for Jacksonville. Never run petting zoo alone. Then cut to slide 6.

**Say:**

Here's the taste test. Same prompt, cold, no login. When the query is mobile goat yoga and mobile petting zoo, we come back number one in Jacksonville.

### Beat 3 · The bad accountant — 2:00–2:40

**Show:** Slide 8 (the turn), or hold on slide 16 (tech anchor).

**Say:**

A year ago people laughed at the consolidation idea. You're a small business, run it like one. That's a bad accountant talking.

I came up in software. I watched perpetual-license companies lose to SaaS companies with a better accountant — better unit economics. Every business down the street has a bad accountant. We bring the good one. And we're already winning with no outside money — just my wife and a few friends — and a big impact in a short time.

### Beat 4 · Not a one-trick pony — 2:40–3:20

**Show:** Slide 12 (ecosystem / flywheel), then slide 9 (complementary founders). Then the product: slide 17 (Growth, the safe screenshot), live [giving-wheel](giving-wheel/) or [gold.html](gold.html), then slide 18 (ops) or [helper-onboarding.html](helper-onboarding.html). Optional: [track.html](track.html) — click Run live demo. Do not linger on live Growth.

**Say:**

This is not a one-trick pony. It's a complementary stacked ecosystem — Critters on Call, STEM on Call, Exec MBA on Call — with a clear purpose, clear problem statements, clear ICPs, and a clearly segmented market. It's beautifully engineered.

And this is the stack that runs it. Dashboards for the book of business. The Gold app. The prize wheel. The helper onboarding so we can run eight gigs at once. That's why it repeats.

### Beat 5 · Enterprise vision — 3:20–3:50

**Show:** Stay on slide 12, or slide 16.

**Say:**

The vision is creative monopolies. Plural. Own the CDD budget, and write it so we win all of them. Local vendors don't get put out of business. They work for us — like Amazon sellers, or like franchisees.

### Beat 6 · Exit — 3:50–4:15

**Show:** Slide 20 (summary).

**Say:**

This is built to exit. Not a lifestyle farm. A tech-enabled experiences and consulting platform — the same roll-up path as other tech-enabled services businesses. Born an enterprise, not molded into one later.

### Beat 7 · The ask — 4:15–5:00

**Show:** Slide 21 (the asks). If they lean in on the SAFE, open [DEAL_SHEET.html?v=1122b](assets/DEAL_SHEET.html?v=1122b).

**Say:**

Three ways to come in.

The first ask is a sponsorship — ride this Black Friday, Cyber Week, and Christmas run-up with us. Kits, petting zoos, flyers. If you have a business, there's advertising in the return. If you're a philanthropist, you're welcome too.

If you want a stake, it's a simple SAFE into On Call Brands — Critters on Call, STEM on Call, Exec MBA on Call. Up to $150,000, a $750,000 post-money cap, a 20% discount. At the cap that's 20%. I keep 80%, and I never go below 51%. The farm stays untouched. This funds formation and access, not a buyout.

And there's a partnership path: the homestead retreat, an incubator village. Trade your rent — a rent-equivalent, something like $3,000 to $5,000 a month, or a down payment — and live on the property in a no-frills, hunting-cabin-style trailer. Or bring your own for glamping. You learn the skills, you help the business, and you become franchise partner zero, owning half of something real — sweat equity. Close four or five of these and you have a high-ticket line.

I'd love to have you with us this season.
