Melesia's playbook

Phone script, voicemail and three emails for each list. Copy, fill the name, send from your own email.

The opener (every call)
Hi, I represent a family business that brings farm animals to events. You're one of the premier programmers in the area. Do you have any dates coming up where bringing animals would make sense?
If they say...
  • "Send me something." Great. Get their email, send email 1 for that list, and attach the Subscription or Violet sheet.
  • "Too expensive." Offer quarterly instead of monthly. You can go to $150 monthly or $200 quarterly. Anything lower, tell them you will check with Michael.
  • "Not right now." Ask which month is better and put a reminder on your calendar.
  • STEM, assemblies, after school, custom. "That one is built to order. Michael will call you with a quote." Then text Michael.
