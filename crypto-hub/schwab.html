Charles Schwab self-directed IRA

Only claims checked against two Schwab pages on Sunday, Oct 4, 2026. This page does not list every product Schwab sells. The catalog is the account, not this page.

No account number, no balance, and no position is on this public page.

Roth and traditional IRAs are self-directed

Schwab's self-directed investing page says Roth and traditional IRAs are self-directed accounts. The same answer calls them some of Schwab's most popular self-directed accounts.

Source: schwab.com/invest-with-us/self-directed-investing

Categories that page names

Under "Choice of investments," Schwab names stocks, exchange-traded funds (ETFs), mutual funds, and options. Those four are the categories named here. Anything else lives in the Schwab account, not on this page.

Source: schwab.com/invest-with-us/self-directed-investing

What an IRA cannot do

Schwab's options article says an investor can't borrow money in an IRA to buy stock. It says an investor can't short stock in an IRA. It says any options trade that requires margin can't be executed in an IRA.

Source: schwab.com/learn/story/trading-options-retirement-account

Limited margin is not a loan

The same article says that when an IRA is approved, it can receive limited margin for certain options spreads, and that this limited margin does not allow the investor to borrow funds to execute trades.

Source: schwab.com/learn/story/trading-options-retirement-account
